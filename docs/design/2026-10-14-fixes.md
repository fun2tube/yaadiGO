## CTO — decisions

| Finding | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| F1 duplicate booking | **One live booking per lead.** Approving a quotation first looks for an open booking on the lead (not cancelled/completed). If there is one, the quotation is attached to it and its money (total, per person, travellers, advance, balance) comes from the quotation; departure and seats stay. Only with no open booking is a new one created. | Two bookings for one trip double revenue in every report; the existing idempotency was per quotation, so a revision (new id) or a prior seat request slipped past it. |
| F8 status on approval | A new quotation booking starts **`requested`**, not `confirmed`. The desk confirms (hotels/transport available), then records the advance. | The quote itself says "confirmed once we receive the advance". `confirmed` also counts as "awaiting payment" money the customer hasn't agreed to pay yet. |
| F7 advance | **One rule: the quotation's payment schedule.** `advanceFromSchedule()` in `quote-content.js` (first row, ₹5,000/person by default) is used by the customer page and by the booking. The last row is now a true *remainder* row (no percentage label). | The 25% rule in `convert.js` disagreed with the customer's own page. |
| F2 offline payments | "Record a payment" form on the booking → existing `POST /api/admin/payments`; ledger listed. A website booking still `requested` must be **confirmed first** (that is what takes the seats under the trip lock); `recordPayment` refuses otherwise. | `requested → part-paid` crosses the seat line without the lock — the same oversell class `setBookingStatus` exists to prevent. |
| F3 GST invoices | Refuse to issue without the seller GSTIN. Every invoice records SAC 998555, seller state, place of supply (state + code), CGST/SGST or IGST split, customer address/GSTIN (optional, validated). Invoice page `/admin/invoices/[id]`, printable. **Credit notes**: own table `credit_notes`, own sequence `YG/CN/<FY>/0001`, amount ≤ invoice − earlier credits, reason required. | A tax invoice without the supplier GSTIN is invalid; immutability needs a reversal path. |
| New: `accounts` role | Add `accounts` to the Postgres `role` enum (migration 0005). | The app offers an Accounts role; Postgres would refuse the insert. |
| F6 scoping | `bookingScope(user)`: people with `team.read`, `services.write` or `invoices.read` see all bookings; everyone else only bookings where `salesOwnerId` is them. Applied to the list page, `GET /api/admin/bookings`, the booking page, payment and payment-link POSTs. Reports/invoices *reading* stays company-wide for Sales (preset decision, unchanged). | Leads were scoped, bookings were not. |
| F5 WhatsApp | All admin `wa.me` links go through `whatsappTo()`. | It already handles 91/trunk prefix. |
| F18 | Day buckets in IST on the server; client label compares calendar days, not rounded 24 h spans. | Vercel runs in UTC; `Math.round` turned "tonight" into "tomorrow". |

## UI/UX — states

- **Record a payment** (booking page, under Collect payment): amount (defaults to the advance if nothing collected, else the outstanding), method, reference, date. States: saving; error inline; overpayment → second confirm; refused because unconfirmed → plain sentence + no form; paid in full → form hidden. Ledger list below: date · method · ref · amount, refunds in red.
- **Invoice desk**: "Raise invoice" opens an inline panel — place of supply (state list, default the seller's state), customer GSTIN (optional), customer address (optional). Missing company GSTIN → banner explaining where to set it, no button. Raised rows link to the invoice page.
- **Invoice page**: A4-styled document, Print / Save as PDF, credit notes listed under it, "Issue credit note" (amount, reason) for invoice writers.
- **Quote builder**: "Days" label; travellers never 0; URL becomes `/admin/quotations/<id>` after first save; sent quotations show no Save/Send, only "Open this quotation".
- Admin screens: no floating site editor bar.

## PM — acceptance criteria

1. Seat request → draft quote → send → approve ⇒ the lead has **one** booking, `requested`, carrying the quote total and the original departure; Reports outstanding equals the quote total.
2. Approving v2 after v1 ⇒ still one booking, now with v2's total.
3. Customer page advance = booking advance = schedule row 1; balance row has no "%".
4. Recording ₹10,000 by bank transfer on a confirmed booking ⇒ Collected ₹10,000, status Part paid, ledger row shown; on a `requested` website booking ⇒ refused with an explanation.
5. Raise invoice with no GSTIN ⇒ refused with the reason. With GSTIN ⇒ invoice shows SAC, CGST/SGST (same state) or IGST (other state), place of supply; page prints. Credit note ⇒ `YG/CN/…/0001`, cannot exceed the invoice.
6. A Sales user sees only their bookings (list, API, page 404s for others).
7. WhatsApp links for a 10-digit number open `wa.me/91…`.
8. Unit tests for each rule; `vitest`, `eslint`, `next build` clean.
